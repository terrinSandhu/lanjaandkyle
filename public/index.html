Welcome to Santa Barbara

Travel

Santa Barbara is a short drive up the coast from LA! If you’re flying in, Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is the easiest option, with the most flight availability. From there, you can rent a car and enjoy the drive up the coast or take the Santa Barbara Airbus.

You can also fly directly into Santa Barbara Airport (SBA), which is much closer to the wedding, but has more limited flight options.

Accommodations

We are currently working on putting together a couple room blocks at various hotels. Information on room blocks will be updated to this website as soon as it’s available. Please check back soon!

Things to do

Make a weekend of it. These are a few of our favorite ways to wander around Santa Barbara.

01 · Go wild

Santa Barbara Zoo

Spend the morning with giraffes and sea lions, then stay for the ocean views from the hilltop.

02 · Sip and stay awhile

Funk Zone drinks and dinner

Start with a tasting room on Anacapa Street, then wander toward the harbor for dinner.

03 · Take a stroll

State Street

Walk the palm-lined blocks for coffee, boutiques, bookstores, and an easy afternoon downtown.

04 · Take the long way

Waterfront biking

Rent bikes near the waterfront and follow the coast toward Butterfly Beach at golden hour.

05 · Find the water

Butterfly Beach

Bring a layer, walk the sand, and stay for the soft light over the mountains across the bay.

06 · A little history

Old Mission and rose garden

Take a quiet loop through the gardens before dinner, with time for the view from the tower.

Good to know

Frequently asked

What is the dress code?

The dress code is black-tie optional. Please wear whatever makes you feel your most beautiful and confident self! Villa Verano has quite a bit of stairs, so comfortable shoes are highly encouraged (no thin high heels).

Are children invited?

While we absolutely love your little ones, this celebration will be adults only.

How do I get to Santa Barbara?

Please see the travel section. The largest airport closest to Santa Barbara is Los Angeles International Airport (LAX). From there, you can rent a car or take the Santa Barbara Airbus. There is also a smaller airport in Santa Barbara (SBA) with limited flight options.

Where should I stay?
